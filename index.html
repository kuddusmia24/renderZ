Welcome to B-CU

A legacy of learning, leadership and service.

Bethune-Cookman University is a student-centered institution where academic development, leadership and community engagement come together.

Founded by Dr. Mary McLeod Bethune, the university continues a tradition of preparing students to become responsible, productive citizens and solution seekers.

"I leave you love. I leave you hope. I leave you the challenge of developing confidence in one another." Dr. Mary McLeod Bethune
